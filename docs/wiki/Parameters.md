# Parameters

Parameters should be adjusted with care. See [Finch et al. (2020). Modelling the functional connectivity of landscapes for greater horseshoe bats Rhinolophus ferrumequinum at a local scale.
](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10980-019-00953-1)

## Source circles

In circuitscape, when considering a roost as a point, current will flow radially inward toward the roost, approximating radially diffusive motion. In Circuitscape, the sink in this case is the roost origin, and the sources are a circle on the boundary or multiple circles further in. For the sink,
voltage is a zero-voltage ground, or a Dirichlet boundary condition. The sources, on the other hand, are Neumann fixed current sources. For a single circle on the boundary, current will be diluted by the large area, and show very little current anywhere except the central pixels. This is resolvable using log-transformed current, which still shows the desired connectivity. Multiple current sources can be used to boost the regions outside of the central point.

## Road and rivers

Road and river parameters include:
- Buffer (M): a buffer around roads is used to compute a mask which contains resistance. I.e. within 5 metres of the road, resistance will contribute.
- Max resistance: the maximum resistance for which roads can contribute.
- Slope: the exponent of the nonlinear resistance found in [1]

## Landscape

Landscape resistance is also governed by a max resistance and slope. Landscape values are governed by a categorical rank derived from [1].

## Linear

Linear resistance is similar to Landscape resistance in that it depends on a categorisation governed by underlying LIDAR DSM/DTM data. See [1].

## Lamp

Lamp resistance is computed in two steps:
- A georeferenced irradiance map is generated by raycasting or inputted directly. Lamp max radius determines the maximum distance light can reach around a lamp. It is a computational convenience.
- Similarly to other features, once the irradiance map is computed, it is converted to resistance via a slope and a max resistance.
